■ Welcome / About Us

Almtmyzwwn for Marketing and PR is a marketing and public relations company based in the heart of Deira, Dubai.

We help companies, brands and individuals reach the right people with the right message. From small shops to big businesses, we offer marketing, advertising, media and PR services at affordable prices.

Our team understands the local market and speaks both Arabic and English.

» Public Relations
Press releases, media relations, reputation management and corporate communication.

» Digital & Social Media
Social media management, content creation, online ads and campaigns.

» Events & Media
Event planning, product launches, press conferences and exhibitions.

» Advertising
Print, outdoor, radio and online advertising for local and regional markets.

» Marketing Consulting
Market research, marketing plans, brand strategy and positioning.

» Branding & Partnerships
Logo and identity, company profiles, brochures, sponsorships and partnerships.
